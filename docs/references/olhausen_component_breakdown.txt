Here's the complete component-by-component breakdown of the Olhausen Remington, with materials and measurements. One honest caveat upfront: Olhausen publishes overall dimensions and the internal engineering specs, but not every single wood member's dimensions — so where exact numbers aren't published, I've marked industry-standard/WPA tournament measurements that apply to this class of table.

---

 Table height is the industry standard ~32" from floor to playing surface (WPA regulation is 29.25"–31"). Assembled weight runs roughly 700–1,000 lbs depending on size — the Tournament Pro 9' (same construction family) weighs 970 lbs .

## 2. Cabinet / Apron
- **Material:** 100% solid kiln-dried hardwood — Maple, Oak, Cherry, or Walnut; sourced from slow-growth Northeastern US trees for tighter grain 
- **Style:** Flat-paneled apron with routered detail 
- **Finish:** UV-cured finish (resists scratching and chipping), up to 35–38 finish options 
- **Hardware:** Steel KD (knock-down) apron brackets — consistent alignment, no visible filler or screws 

## 3. Legs
- **Material:** Solid hardwood, matched to the cabinet wood/finish
- **Design:** **Floor-to-slate construction** — the legs run uninterrupted from the floor up to directly beneath the slate, carrying the slate's full load vertically rather than through the frame 
- **Hardware:** Adjustable levelers/anchor plates at floor contact points

## 4. Frame / Slate Support (the engineering core)
- **Uniliner® slate support body:** Solid wood integrated liner/frame that supports a larger slate area, keeps slate flat and level, minimizes wood movement from humidity, and is nearly **twice as strong as length center braces**; frame strength tested by Weyerhaeuser to support up to **27,000 lbs** 
- **Slate liner:** Solid wood framing around the slate perimeter — dampens vibration for quieter play and protects the slate in transport 
- **Bolt-thru crossbrace:** Steel bolts physically support the braces, eliminating frame sag/spread and climate-driven movement 

## 5. Slate (the playing bed)
- **Material:** Natural slate stone, **diamond-honed** for flatness; each piece rigorously inspected for flatness and bolt-hole placement 
- **Configuration:** **3-piece, 1" thick** (1.25" available as upgrade) 
- **Weight:** Each piece **~200+ lbs** — roughly 600+ lbs of slate per table 
- **Joint treatment:** Seams filled and leveled with auto body filler or wax, then wet-sanded flush during installation

## 6. Rails (top rail assemblies)
- **Material:** Solid hardwood, same species as cabinet, UV finished 
- **Depth:** ~5" industry-standard rail depth 
- **Attachment:** **T-Nut rail construction** — steel T-nuts embedded in the rails accept long-grip bolts from beneath; eliminates dead spots, won't bend or cross-thread, allows precise alignment and easy removal for re-covering 
- **Sights:** Inlaid mother-of-pearl or diamond-shaped sights (configurable)

## 7. Cushions (bumpers)
- **Material:** **Accu-Fast® — 100% pure natural gum rubber**, American made, 100% bonded to the rail 
- **Profile:** True **K-66** — height **1 3/16"**, top/face width **1 1/8"**, 66° cross-section angle 
- **Nose height (contact point):** 1 3/8"–1 7/16" above the playfield — the WPA spec for 2.25" balls 
- **Length:** 6 cushions total, ~48" stock cut to rail size 
- **Warranty:** Lifetime — unlike extruded rubber with fillers, pure gum rubber doesn't harden and crack 

## 8. Pockets
- **Material:** Leather — **fringe or shield style** (go shield for the premium look) 
- **Facings:** Rubber/neoprene pocket facings where the cushion meets the pocket mouth
- **Corner pocket openings:** 4.5"–4.625" (WPA standard range 4.5"–4.75") 
- **Side pocket openings:** 5"–5.125" 
- **Hardware:** Steel pocket irons underneath; **no ball return** — drop pockets only 
- One known trait from the pro community: Olhausen pockets run a wider opening-to-throat ratio, which can produce the occasional "rattle" on balls hit hard along the rail into corners 

## 9. Cloth (felt)
- **Stock:** Championship or Simonis brand 
- **Upgrade (do it):** **Simonis 860** — worsted wool (90% wool / 10% nylon), nap-free weave, ~21 oz; available in 26 colors 
- **Bed cloth:** cut to playfield + 6" overhang per side; rail cloth strips per rail

## 10. Hardware & Fasteners
- Steel T-nuts and bolts for rail assembly 
- Steel KD apron brackets 
- Steel bolt-thru crossbrace hardware 
- Slate-to-frame screws with recessed heads

## 11. Included Accessories
- Four cues, bridge stick (58" standard cue length ), wall-mounted cue rack, ball set (2.25" diameter phenolic resin), 8-ball and 9-ball racks, chalk, table cover, repair kit, table brushes
